# Synthetic case manifest

## Disclosure

**Synthetic / simulated project case.** No document represents a real M Moser project, client, employee, building, or professional recommendation. The case exists to test a Human–AI workflow and does not claim architectural validation.

## Scenario

Aurora & Field is a fictional professional-services organisation bringing two previously separate practices into a shared workplace. The initial project narrative emphasises a contemporary client experience, visible collaboration, and a unified identity. As research and reviews progress, the team receives new information about cultural continuity, confidential work, and how knowledge is actually exchanged.

The experiment tests whether the system preserves the reasoning behind earlier intents and decisions, then surfaces only plausible review triggers as the project understanding changes.
