# S-003 — Concept review and decision record

**Version:** V1  
**Status:** synthetic approved project record  
**Date:** 2 February 2026

## Approved design intents

**I-001 — Purposeful exchange**  
Create more opportunities for different disciplines to encounter and develop each other's work.

**I-002 — Legible expertise**  
Help employees and visitors understand the organisation's combined expertise through the workplace experience.

**I-003 — Shared identity**  
Create a recognisable common experience while the organisation integrates two established practices.

## Working assumptions

**A-001**  
More frequent informal encounters will lead to earlier and more useful knowledge exchange between disciplines.

**A-002**  
Visual openness can express collaboration without materially limiting confidential client work.

**A-003**  
A consistent visual and spatial language will help employees experience the organisation as unified.

## Early design decisions

**D-001 — Central review setting**  
Develop a prominent shared setting for informal discussion and display of work in progress. Informed by I-001 and A-001.

**D-002 — Visible client journey**  
Explore transparent or visually connected boundaries along parts of the client-facing journey. Informed by I-002 and A-002.

**D-003 — Common expression**  
Develop a consistent common design language across shared settings. Informed by I-003 and A-003.

## Open questions

- Which work activities benefit from informal encounter?
- Where does confidentiality require visual or acoustic separation?
- Which existing practices should remain visibly distinct?
