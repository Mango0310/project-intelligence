# S-004 — Culture workshop synthesis

**Version:** V2  
**Status:** synthetic research synthesis  
**Date:** 18 February 2026

Participants from both former practices were asked what they wanted to preserve and what they wanted to change. Several groups described specialised review rituals, material libraries, and team stories as important sources of professional identity. Participants generally welcomed a stronger shared organisation, but several associated “one common look” with losing evidence of how their practice works.

The workshop supports a distinction between organisational unity and visual uniformity. It does not establish which design response should follow.

## Method note

This is a facilitated workshop synthesis. It records recurring views in the session but is not a representative employee survey.
