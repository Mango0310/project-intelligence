# S-001 — Initial client brief

**Version:** V1  
**Status:** synthetic source  
**Date:** 12 January 2026

Aurora & Field wants its new shared workplace to signal that the organisation has entered a new chapter following the integration of two practices. Leadership wants clients to experience the organisation as contemporary, connected, and confident.

The workplace should make collaboration more visible and reduce the sense that expertise is held inside separate teams. The arrival and client-facing journey should communicate a coherent identity. Leadership would like the design team to explore a central setting where work in progress can be encountered and discussed.

The project must continue to support confidential client work. The organisation also intends to retain and reuse suitable existing materials where this is consistent with the eventual design direction.

At this stage, the brief does not define what kinds of collaboration are currently failing, how different teams exchange knowledge, or how employees from the two former practices understand a unified identity.
